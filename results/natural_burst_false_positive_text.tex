\paragraph{False-positive check on natural low-rating bursts.} Using the non-injected Uber analysis split, we identified 23 text-diverse hourly low-rating burst bucket(s) without using attack labels. Natural-burst reviews retained a median base contribution weight of 0.648 (mean 0.597), compared with 0.779 (mean 0.639) for other low-rating reviews and 0.176 (mean 0.272) for the injected 20\% coordinated-attack rows. Only 14.1\% of natural-burst reviews had $w<0.3$, versus 67.4\% of injected rows. This diagnostic does not establish spam ground truth; it indicates that a temporally concentrated low-rating event with diverse wording is not downweighted to the same extent as the coordinated injected stream.
